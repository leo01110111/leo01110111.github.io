There are only vibes in this world. We just call the vibes that have survived for thousands of years “truth”.

While I’m not sure if there’s truth in this world, I know I can be less wrong.

While I’m not sure if there’s a good life, I know there are good things and I should fill my life with them.

Conventional wisdom of which I often get on podcasts, are heuristics. They're not always right. They are just approximations that have helped the person act in a desirable way. Imagine life as a chess game. You can't quite know what sequence of moves leads to victory, but you have a better idea of how to makes moves to control the center or take the opponent's pieces.

I have found that everybody has an interesting heuristic to share, if I can understand where they're coming from.

Heuristics come in a coarse grains. The general discourse on X follows a coarse grains, the Dwarkesh Podcast follows a coarse grain. 

Heuristics won't change your life unless you apply them, which is difficult.

Heuristics are approximations that only apply in certain situations, so don't take them that seriously. I like to think that the only true things in this world is math and science. Given that, I have started to realize just how much freedom I have in this world. And even math and science is incomplete.
